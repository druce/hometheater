⏺ I've written setup-and-calibration.md (1,005 words) and committed it. It's
  linked from the recommendation, the buying checklist and the index. No step
  has been tried in your room yet, so the page is marked unverified.

  The order it sets for the X3900H with one sub:

  1. Place the sub first. Its position changes the room's bass itself, while
     correction only changes the signal. The first spot to try is the middle of
     a wall, which a 2006 Harman study (Welti and Devantier) found best in
     rectangular rooms. Turn the sub's own crossover knob to maximum so the
     receiver does the filtering.
  2. Speaker settings. Set up as 5.1 with an 80 Hz crossover until your speakers
     are chosen, and set Subwoofer Output to LFE. Changing the speaker layout
     later switches off both Audyssey and Dirac until you re-run them.
  3. Audyssey XT32 into Preset 1. This is your free baseline: six or more mic
     positions within about 2 ft of the main seat. Try both the Reference and
     Flat curves; Denon suggests Flat for small rooms.
  4. Dirac Room Correction and Bass Control into Preset 2.
     - Keep the mic positions close to the seat. In Ethan Winer's measurements
       the benefit faded within about 2 ft.
     - Cut peaks rather than boosting dips, and keep any boost to about 5 dB.
     - You may need to buy a USB measurement mic (open question #19).
  5. Compare. Switch between the two presets at the same volume and on the same
     tracks. No source measures XT32 against Dirac with one sub, so your result
     is new evidence for the wiki.
  6. Music in Stereo mode, not Pure Direct. Pure Direct and Direct switch off
     all room correction, and with this setup they also drop the bass below the
     crossover.
  7. WiiM Pro on coax. Turn Fixed Volume on and EQ off, leave RoomFit off, then
     run the Radio Paradise tests from the buying checklist.

  The "What this doesn't cover" section lists what's left out: crossovers for
  your specific speakers, which wait on that choice; how a second sub would
  change the steps; and calibration for the stereo rooms.

  What the wiki says now

  - Streaming & multiroom hub: the receiver's own app (HEOS) becomes a second
    app next to WiiM Home unless the whole house runs on one platform.
    - WiiM speakers can join Google Cast or Alexa groups. But a physical input,
      such as the TV, can only be shared within an all-WiiM group.
    - The Amp Ultra has no AirPlay 2, so an iPhone can't group it with the
      receiver.
  - My system: a WiiM Amp Ultra ($549) for each stereo room.
  - Sonos: almost nothing. The build register records that Radio Paradise says
    Sonos plays its FLAC stream natively, and the Onkyo supports "Works with
    Sonos" through a Sonos Port. There are no Sonos prices, models or limits.

  The 9.1 idea: the X3900H can power only one other room

  The manual (pp.157–163, 214) shows the X3900H has a Zone 2 but no Zone 3, so
  it can't drive two extra rooms on its own.

  Powered Zone 2:
  - Set "Amp Assign" to "7.1ch + ZONE2", which is the default. Zone 2 then plays
    from the Height 2 speaker terminals.
  - Your 5.1 main room is unaffected.
  - What it needs: a speaker-wire run from the receiver to that room's speakers.

  Other Zone 2 outputs:
  - A Zone 2 pre-out, to feed an amp in that room.
  - An HDMI Zone 2 output, for a second TV.
  - These are other ways to reach the same zone, not an extra zone.

  What Zone 2 can play:
  - The same source as the main room, or a different one.
  - Sources: HEOS, analog, USB, the tuner, and the coax or optical inputs
    (two-channel PCM only).
  - So the WiiM Pro's Radio Paradise stream on coax can play in Zone 2.
  - HDMI sources need Zone 2's "HDMI Audio" set to PCM.
  - If both zones play the same HDMI source, the main room may drop to
    two-channel PCM as well (manual p.253).

  The manual doesn't settle these points, so they're unverified:
  - whether room correction applies in Zone 2;
  - whether Zone 2 needs the main receiver switched on.

  Other receivers: the Onkyo RZ50 has Zone 2 and Zone 3, but Zone 3 is a line
  output that needs its own amp. The Yamaha RX-A4A can assign two amp channels
  to Zone 2.

  ### Key findings
  All page numbers below are from the X3900H manual
  (r2-denon-avr-x3900h-manual-zone2.md) unless another source is named.

  **Amp Assign**
  - Only two of the ten modes power Zone 2: 7.1ch + ZONE2 and 5.1ch (Bi-Amp) +
  ZONE2 (p.46, p.215).
  - 7.1ch + ZONE2 is the factory default.
  - A 5.1 main room "Can be set in all 'Amp Assign' settings" (p.46). The owner
  can therefore keep the default with only 5.1 connected; bi-amping the fronts
  is not required to get Zone 2.
  - In both modes, powered Zone 2 comes from the HEIGHT2 speaker terminals
  (p.158, p.228). Those amps are rated 105 W per channel into 8 Ω, two channels
  driven (p.329). There is no Zone 3 anywhere in the manual.

  **Zone 2 connections**
  - A ZONE2 PRE OUT pair exists (p.27, p.253). Its output level is not stated.
  - Volume Level can be Variable (the default) or fixed at a value from 1 to 98
  (p.253).
  - HDMI ZONE2 OUT carries only HDMI inputs 1–4; HDMI 5 and 6 are not supported
  (p.158, p.289). It does not work properly with HDMI Control (CEC) turned on
  (p.144).

  **Zone 2 sources** (p.163)
  - Sources: HDMI, coax/optical, analog, USB, HEOS Music and tuner.
  - Coax and optical carry 2-channel PCM only.
  - HDMI needs ZONE2 Setup > HDMI Audio set to "PCM" for anything other than
  2-channel PCM.
  - Bluetooth in Zone 2 appears only in troubleshooting (p.276), not in the
  source tables. The manual is inconsistent here.

  **The WiiM on coax can feed Zone 2.** It qualifies under the coax rule because
  it outputs 2-channel PCM; the manual does not name WiiM, so that link is my
  inference. This works whether Zone 2 plays a separate source or mirrors the
  main zone.

  **Same source in both rooms**
  - **TV Audio Sharing** keeps the main room's surround format (e.g. Atmos from
  eARC) while Zone 2 plays the same content (p.160). It is set by choosing
  "Source" as the Zone 2 input. "Source" is the default but can be selected only
  from the front-panel ZONE2 SOURCE button.
  - Selecting the same HDMI input by name in both zones with HDMI Audio = PCM,
  or using HDMI ZONE2 OUT on the same input, pushes the main zone to 2-channel
  PCM (p.253, p.158, p.289).
  - **All Zone Stereo** limits the main room to the Stereo and Multi Ch Stereo
  modes (p.127).

  **Zone 2 processing** (p.252)
  - Available: bass and treble (±10 dB), left and right level (±12 dB),
  stereo/mono, and an on/off high-pass filter with no corner frequency stated.
  - Not mentioned anywhere: Audyssey or Dirac in Zone 2, a crossover, or a Zone
  2 subwoofer. Treat Zone 2 as uncorrected, though the manual does not
  explicitly exclude correction.

  **Zone 2 volume** (p.253, p.164)
  - The volume limit ships at 70 (−10 dB) and can be set from 60 to 80 or Off.
  - Power On Volume options are Last (the default), Mute or a fixed 1–98.
  - Mute level options are Full, −40 dB or −20 dB (p.254).

  **Zone 2 power** (p.270, p.250, p.241)
  - Zone 2 keeps running when the main zone is turned off: the unit stays on and
  shows "ZONE2 On".
  - Zone 2 has its own auto-standby (8 h, 4 h, 2 h, or Off by default).
  - Using the HEOS app with the unit in standby requires Network Control =
  Always On, which raises standby power by an amount the manual does not state.
  - ECO Mode behaviour and power draw with Zone 2 are not stated. Rated
  consumption is 660 W, and 0.1 W in standby only with Network Control off
  (p.333).

  **HEOS app**
  - The manual does not say whether Zone 2 appears as its own player or can join
  a HEOS group, and does not say whether Main and Zone 2 can play two different
  HEOS streams at once.
  - The Yamaha RX-A4A guide states the opposite case outright: NET, USB and
  Bluetooth sources cannot be chosen separately per zone (p.167).

  **Sharing a physical input to another HEOS device**
  - The X3900H manual says nothing about it.
  - The HEOS CLI spec v1.10 has a play_input command that can send a source
  device's input, coax included, to another player. An input can go to one
  player or one group only, and cannot be shared while the source device is
  playing that input itself (r2-heos-cli-spec-input-sharing.md).
  - If current firmware still behaves this way, the main room and the kitchen
  speaker cannot play the coax-connected WiiM together.

  - Zone 2 takes HDMI (inputs 1–3), S/PDIF and network sources, with digital

  **Key findings**

  *Port into the receiver*
  - **Price and outputs:** The Port is $499. Its outputs are analog RCA plus
  digital coax; there is no optical and no HDMI. It has an auto-detecting analog
  RCA line-in, and Sonos specifies THD+N <0.009% from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, with no
  test conditions given. Sources: r2-sonos-port-product-page.md,
  r2-sonos-port-product-guide.md, r2-sonos-port-setup-support.md.
  - **Output level modes:** The line-out has three settings: Fixed (volume and
  EQ from the receiver only), Variable (volume from the Sonos app) and
  Pass-Through (the Port stays at fixed level and sends volume commands to
  "amplifiers that support pass-through"). Sonos does not say which amplifiers
  support it. Sources: the Port guide and the setup article.
  - **No input switching on the receiver:** Two Sonos pages tell the user to set
  the receiver to the Port's input by hand: "Select the proper input channel on
  the receiver" (Port guide) and "make sure your amplifier or receiver is set
  to the input that Port is connected to" (setup article).
  - **The 12V trigger cannot work with the X3900H:** The Port's trigger is an
  output only, rated 12 V/200 mA, and it only wakes an amplifier that has a 12V
  trigger input. The X3900H has a TRIGGER OUT jack only (manual p.75, already in
  extracts/denon-avr-x3900h-owners-manual-na.md), so there is nothing to
  connect it to. Sources: setup article and Port guide.
  - **No Trueplay:** The Port does not support Trueplay. Source:
  r2-sonos-trueplay-support-articles.md.
