# My system: requirements

**The receiver has to run a 5.1 system with no sub owned yet in a small closed room, and play Radio Paradise FLAC, Spotify and Apple Music natively. It should also grow into a four-room setup later.** Every ranking in this wiki is relative to this page. Change it here first when a requirement changes.

## Captured 2026-11-08 (planning interview)

| Area | Requirement | Consequence for the receiver |
|---|---|---|
| Layout | 5.1 now; 5.2 possible | 7 amp channels are plenty. Two independently EQ'd sub outputs are valuable insurance, since the sub(s) aren't bought yet. |
| Subwoofer | None owned; choosing one is in scope | Room correction must integrate the sub well (bass management, sub/main alignment). Whether a second sub ever gets added should be decided together with the receiver. |
| Room | Small closed room (under about 2,500 cu ft) | Amp power is unlikely to bind with typical speakers. Room modes will dominate bass, so room correction quality matters more than watts. |
| Budget | Open | Rank on merit first, then price each step up against what it buys. |
| Priorities | Room correction; music & streaming | Criteria 1, 2 and 3 carry the most weight in the decision. |
| Music sources | Radio Paradise lossless FLAC; Spotify; Apple Music | Spotify Connect and AirPlay 2 are table stakes. Native Radio Paradise FLAC is a discriminating requirement (see below). |
| Multiroom, now | One room only | Not a factor today. |
| Multiroom, future | Two more rooms with stereo speakers, plus one wireless speaker each in the kitchen and bathroom | The streaming ecosystem has to extend to four more rooms. A platform's range of standalone speakers and amps matters as much as Zone 2. |

## Radio Paradise FLAC: why it discriminates

Radio Paradise publishes several lossless (CD-quality FLAC) mixes, the main one at `stream.radioparadise.com/flac` ([stereonet thread](https://stereonet.com/forums/topic/516085-great-sounding-internet-radio-stations), unverified against Radio Paradise's own page). Support depends on the platform. These are first-pass findings, all unverified, and they need primary-source checks during ingest:

- **BluOS:** the lossless stream is reported as available natively, as it is on Sonos ([stereonet](https://stereonet.com/forums/topic/516085-great-sounding-internet-radio-stations)).
- **HEOS (Denon, Marantz):** owners reach Radio Paradise through TuneIn, and TuneIn reportedly does not carry the FLAC stream ([eCoustics interview with William Goldsmith](https://www.ecoustics.com/podcasts/radio-paradise-william-goldsmith/)). Native FLAC may need Roon, a URL-capable app or an external streamer.
- **Roon:** supports FLAC internet radio, but owners report Radio Paradise falling back to AAC ([Roon community](https://community.roonlabs.com/t/radio-paradise-flac-link-producing-limited-aac-stream-of-approx-350kbps/314664)). The dates are unclear, so this may be fixed.
- **MusicCast (Yamaha), Onkyo/Pioneer, Anthem (Google Cast), Sony:** unknown.

An external streamer (for example a BluOS Node or WiiM) can feed any receiver and meets this requirement on its own. Whether the requirement is "native on the receiver" or "anywhere in the chain" is still open, and it changes the ranking.
