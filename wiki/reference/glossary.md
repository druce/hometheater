# Glossary

**Each term used across the wiki is defined here in one line, as the wiki's own pages define it, and linked to the page that explains it.**

## Terms

- **airable** — the station directory behind Yamaha's NET RADIO input; the codec it carries for Radio Paradise is unstated. [MusicCast](../systems/musiccast.md)
- **AirPlay 2** — Apple protocol in which an iPhone, iPad or Mac sends the audio to one or several devices in sync. [Streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md)
- **ALAC** — Apple's lossless format, offered by the Radio Paradise iOS app alongside AAC. [Lossless streaming paths](../concepts/lossless-streaming-paths.md)
- **ARC Genesis** — Anthem's free Windows/Mac room correction; on the MRX it sets crossover, sub level and phase but not speaker distances. [ARC Genesis](../systems/arc-genesis.md)
- **ART (Active Room Treatment)** — Dirac's top licence, driving all speakers to cancel room resonances; it requires Room Correction, and, with a sub, Bass Control. [Dirac Live](../systems/dirac-live.md)
- **Associated channels** — the FTC rule's channels that must be driven for a rated power figure, at minimum the two fronts. [Amplifier power ratings](../concepts/amplifier-power-ratings.md)
- **Bass Control** — Dirac add-on licence that computes crossovers, sub distance and level and time-aligns the sub; it requires Room Correction. [Dirac Live](../systems/dirac-live.md)
- **Bass management** — high-passing each speaker at its crossover and redirecting the bass below it to the sub, separately from the LFE channel. [Bass management and crossovers](../concepts/bass-management-and-crossovers.md)
- **Bass Sync** — Denon setting that delays only the LFE track of multichannel discs; it does not align the sub. [Bass management and crossovers](../concepts/bass-management-and-crossovers.md)
- **Bit-perfect** — output that carries the source samples unchanged; a WiiM delivers it only with Fixed Volume on and EQ off. [Lossless streaming paths](../concepts/lossless-streaming-paths.md)
- **Bluetooth (SBC, AAC)** — the lossy fallback path. [Streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md)
- **BluOS** — Lenbrook's streaming and multiroom platform on Bluesound and NAD, the only platform here with Radio Paradise documented as built in. [BluOS](../systems/bluos.md)
- **Coax (S/PDIF)** — electrical digital input carrying PCM from an external streamer; the RX-A4A and AVA15 state 192 kHz on coax. [Lossless streaming paths](../concepts/lossless-streaming-paths.md)
- **Compressed Music Enhancer** — Yamaha processing switched on by default for network, USB and Bluetooth inputs. [MusicCast](../systems/musiccast.md)
- **Connect (Spotify, TIDAL, Qobuz)** — the phone acts as a remote and the device streams from the service itself. [Streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md)
- **Crossover** — the frequency below which a speaker's bass goes to the sub; 80 Hz is the Denon and Yamaha default. [Bass management and crossovers](../concepts/bass-management-and-crossovers.md)
- **DCAC IX** — Sony's included auto-calibration, two measurements at one position against three fixed targets; no candidate carries it. [Room correction](../criteria/room-correction.md)
- **Digital Passthrough** — BluOS mode sending digital output to an external DAC, capped at 24-bit/96 kHz on the NODE and VAULT. [BluOS](../systems/bluos.md)
- **Dirac Live** — licensed room correction sold as three stacking licences (Room Correction, Bass Control, ART). [Dirac Live](../systems/dirac-live.md)
- **Dynamic EQ** — Audyssey's volume-dependent loudness compensation, on by default. [Audyssey MultEQ](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md)
- **eARC** — the enhanced HDMI audio return channel from the TV; an HDMI output with eARC is the connectivity minimum here. [Connectivity](../criteria/connectivity.md)
- **ECO Mode** — Denon and Yamaha power-saving setting that lowers the supply rail and maximum output; Off is advised for loud playback. [Amplifier power ratings](../concepts/amplifier-power-ratings.md)
- **Extra Bass** — Yamaha feature that copies bass from Large mains to the sub; Audioholics advises leaving it off. [YPAO](../systems/ypao.md)
- **FLAC** — lossless audio format; Radio Paradise publishes a free FLAC stream for each channel. [Lossless streaming paths](../concepts/lossless-streaming-paths.md)
- **flacbg** — Radio Paradise FLAC variant "processed for background listening" in an undocumented way. [Radio Paradise](../systems/radio-paradise.md)
- **FTC power rating (16 CFR 432)** — continuous power with the associated channels driven, into 8 ohms, 20 Hz–20 kHz, at no more than 1% THD+N. [Amplifier power ratings](../concepts/amplifier-power-ratings.md)
- **Google Cast** — a Cast-enabled app tells the device what to play; not listed for Denon, Marantz or the RX-A4A. [Streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md)
- **HEOS** — Denon and Marantz streaming layer; its internet radio plays MP3, WMA and AAC only, and Wi-Fi features need an account. [HEOS](../systems/heos.md)
- **HEOS CLI** — local control protocol whose `play_stream` command takes a stream URL. [HEOS](../systems/heos.md)
- **LFC (Low Frequency Containment)** — XT32-only Audyssey function that removes bass passing through walls and adds psychoacoustic bass. [Audyssey MultEQ](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md)
- **LFE** — the ".1" channel authored by the mixer, with its own low-pass filter (70–250 Hz, default 120 Hz, on the X3900H). [Bass management and crossovers](../concepts/bass-management-and-crossovers.md)
- **LFE + Main** — Denon Subwoofer Output setting that also copies the low bass of Full Range speakers into the sub. [Bass management and crossovers](../concepts/bass-management-and-crossovers.md)
- **Limited vs Full bandwidth** — Dirac Room Correction tiers correcting 20–500 Hz and 20–20,000 Hz respectively. [Dirac Live](../systems/dirac-live.md)
- **Low-impedance setting** — selector such as Yamaha's 6Ω MIN; bench reviewers report it cuts power, with no manufacturer document saying so. [Amplifier power ratings](../concepts/amplifier-power-ratings.md)
- **MQA** — a packaged format a certified player must unfold; Radio Paradise's BluOS stream unfolds only inside the BluOS player. [Lossless streaming paths](../concepts/lossless-streaming-paths.md)
- **MultEQ Editor** — Audyssey's paid iOS app for editing target curves per channel pair on Denon and Marantz receivers. [Audyssey MultEQ](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md)
- **MultEQ XT / XT32** — Audyssey's built-in tiers; XT32 ("Platinum") has higher filter resolution plus LFC and SubEQ HT, XT ("Silver") neither. [Audyssey MultEQ](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md)
- **MultEQ-X** — Windows Audyssey app with up to 32 positions and per-channel targets, licensed per receiver serial; 2026-model support is unconfirmed. [Audyssey MultEQ](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md)
- **MusicCast** — Yamaha's free streaming and multiroom platform; linked rooms are down-converted to 48 kHz. [MusicCast](../systems/musiccast.md)
- **MusicCast Surround** — wireless surrounds and sub for a Yamaha receiver; it mutes wired surround outputs and disables multipoint YPAO. [MusicCast](../systems/musiccast.md)
- **Null** — a cancellation at one position in the room; boosting it costs headroom, raises distortion and can lengthen ringing. [Room modes and the limits of correction](../concepts/room-modes-and-correction-limits.md)
- **Open Network Stream** — WiiM route that owners report for playing direct station URLs; WiiM documents no such feature. [WiiM](../systems/wiim.md)
- **Optical (Toslink)** — optical digital input; ASR measures receiver SINAD through it, and the RX-A4A and AVA15 state 96 kHz on it. [Measurements and audibility](../concepts/measurements-and-audibility.md)
- **Paralleled sub outputs** — two sub outputs carrying one signal, as on the MRX 540 and RX-A4A; with one sub this costs nothing. [ARC Genesis](../systems/arc-genesis.md)
- **Power limiter ("limp mode")** — latched output limiting ASR measured across Onkyo/Pioneer receivers, cleared only by pulling the AC cord. [Amplifier power ratings](../concepts/amplifier-power-ratings.md)
- **Pre-out** — line-level RCA output for a channel, letting an external amplifier take over that channel later. [Amplification & pre-outs](../criteria/amplification-and-preouts.md)
- **Pure Direct / Pure Audio** — minimum-processing modes; Denon's Direct and Pure Direct switch off Audyssey and Dirac, Onkyo's Pure Audio bypasses bass management and Dirac. [Bass management and crossovers](../concepts/bass-management-and-crossovers.md)
- **Q** — the width of an EQ filter; Winer advises lower-Q filters aimed at peaks rather than nulls. [Room modes and the limits of correction](../concepts/room-modes-and-correction-limits.md)
- **Radio Paradise** — listener-supported internet station with no ads or subscription, publishing FLAC, AAC and MP3 streams per channel. [Radio Paradise](../systems/radio-paradise.md)
- **Ringing (decay time)** — how long a room resonance keeps sounding; Winer measured at most a 1.6 dB reduction from Dirac. [Room modes and the limits of correction](../concepts/room-modes-and-correction-limits.md)
- **Room mode** — a standing wave set by the room's dimensions, producing fixed peaks and nulls that vary seat to seat. [Room modes and the limits of correction](../concepts/room-modes-and-correction-limits.md)
- **RoomFit** — WiiM's magnitude-only parametric EQ toward a chosen target, measured with a phone or external mic. [WiiM](../systems/wiim.md)
- **Roon Ready** — certification for a device acting as an endpoint for a Roon Core, which needs its own computer and subscription; distinct from Onkyo's "Roon Tested". [Streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md)
- **SINAD** — ratio of a test signal to noise plus distortion, THD+N in dB; ASR calls it an engineering-quality measure, not a perceptual one. [Measurements and audibility](../concepts/measurements-and-audibility.md)
- **Speaker Preset** — Denon and Marantz memory for layout, distances, levels and correction data; Audyssey uses Preset 1, Dirac Preset 2. [Audyssey MultEQ](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md)
- **Spotify Connect** — the Connect protocol for Spotify; Denon supports free and Premium accounts on it. [Streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md)
- **SubEQ HT** — XT32 function that levels and time-aligns up to four subs; it does nothing for one sub. [Audyssey MultEQ](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md)
- **Target curve** — the response a correction system aims for, built in or hand-drawn on Dirac and fixed to three curves in Audyssey's built-in menus. [Dirac Live](../systems/dirac-live.md)
- **THD+N** — total harmonic distortion plus noise as a percentage of output; the FTC rating allows up to 1%. [Measurements and audibility](../concepts/measurements-and-audibility.md)
- **TuneIn** — station directory used for HEOS internet radio; Radio Paradise says the route lacks skip and its highest quality. [Radio Paradise](../systems/radio-paradise.md)
- **WiiM Home** — WiiM's app for its streamers and streaming amplifiers. [WiiM](../systems/wiim.md)
- **YPAO** — Yamaha's included multipoint room correction; its Low Frequency mode detects bass problem frequencies but leaves Q and gain to the user. [YPAO](../systems/ypao.md)
