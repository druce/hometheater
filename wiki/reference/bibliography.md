# Bibliography

**Of the 123 sources registered in `raw/manifest.txt`, the 119 that wiki pages cite are listed here, and seven of them carry most of the recommendation.**

Entries give author or publisher, title, date as registered, the file in `raw/`, and the source URL where one exists. Dates marked "(best guess)" could not be confirmed when the page was captured. Prices in vendor pages were observed on 2026-11-08 and age quickly. Four registered sources that no page cites are left out.

## Read these first

- Amir Majidimehr, ASR, *Denon AVR-X3900H AVR Review*, 2026-09-17 — `asr-denon-avr-x3900h-review.md`: the only bench test of the recommended receiver, and the basis for its first place on music performance.
- Denon, *AVR-X3900H Owner's Manual (NA)*, 2026-04-06 — `denon-avr-x3900h-owners-manual-na.pdf`: the most-cited source in the wiki, for Dirac and Audyssey presets, modes that bypass correction, sub outputs and internet-radio formats.
- Dirac Research, *Dirac Live Room Correction* (licence tiers, X3900H context), 2026-11 — `dirac-live-room-correction-license-tiers.md`: what Limited and Full correct and what each costs; read with the X3900H device page, `dirac-live-upgrade-denon-avr-x3900h-pricing.md`.
- Amir Majidimehr, ASR, *Onkyo TX-RZ50 Review*, 2022-02-12 — `asr-onkyo-tx-rz50-review.md`: the 4-ohm limiter measurement that decides whether the cheaper alternative is viable.
- Onkyo, *TX-RZ50 Firmware Update Notes*, 2026-03-03 — `onkyo-tx-rz50-firmware-update-2026-03.pdf`: four years of Dirac fixes on the RZ50, and silence on the limiter.
- Todd Welti and Allan Devantier, *Low-Frequency Optimization Using Multiple Subwoofers*, J. AES, 2006-05 — `v-welti-devantier-multisub-aes2006.pdf`: why a single sub leaves large seat-to-seat bass differences that correction calibrated at one seat cannot fix.
- WiiM Help Center, *Is Apple Music Supported?* and *How to Set Up Your WiiM Ultra*, 2025–2026 — `v-wiim-faq-apple-music.md`, `v-wiim-faq-wiim-ultra-setup.md`: what WiiM itself documents (Apple Music over AirPlay at 44.1/16; the Ultra's digital outputs), against which the owner-reported Radio Paradise lossless playback has to be read.
