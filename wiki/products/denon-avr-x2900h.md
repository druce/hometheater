# Denon AVR-X2900H

**At $1,349 the X2900H is the cheapest new Denon candidate and runs 5.1 with amps to spare, but it ships the lower MultEQ XT, cannot take Dirac Bass Control, and has never been independently measured.**

Ranked in: [Room correction & bass management](../criteria/room-correction.md) · [Music & two-channel performance](../criteria/music-performance.md) · [Streaming & multiroom](../criteria/streaming-and-multiroom.md) · [Amplification & pre-outs](../criteria/amplification-and-preouts.md) · [Connectivity](../criteria/connectivity.md) · [Usability, app & firmware support](../criteria/usability-and-support.md) · [Value, reliability & resale](../criteria/value-and-reliability.md).

## Verdict

Provisional: the X2900H's best case is a buyer content with Audyssey, or with Dirac Room Correction alone, who wants the HEOS platform for the future rooms at the lowest Denon price. Full-bandwidth Dirac brings it to $1,698. Its main problem for this room is the sub: with Bass Control unavailable, the single subwoofer is integrated by Audyssey XT or by hand, which removes the feature the room-correction criterion weighs most for one sub. No measurement exists to set against the X3900H's.

Two points stay open until the X2900H manual is captured (open question #21). "Pre-out 2.2" most plausibly means front left and right plus two sub outputs, which would rule out adding an external amplifier for the center and surrounds; that reading is an inference. Whether there is a Zone 2 HDMI output, as on the X3900H, is not stated. The single coax input is enough for a WiiM on coax. Rating conventions are explained in [power ratings](../concepts/amplifier-power-ratings.md).

## Room correction

[Audyssey MultEQ XT](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md), Denon's "Silver" tier below XT32, is included. The $19.99 iOS MultEQ Editor app lists the X2900H; the Windows MultEQ-X list does not (both captured 2026-11-08).

Only Room Correction is offered from [Dirac Live](../systems/dirac-live.md), at $259–$349; Bass Control and ART are marked not supported (Dirac grid and device page, 2026-11-08), and Denon's product page and info sheet name Room Correction only. With Room Correction alone, crossover, sub distance and sub level are set by hand rather than computed; the Dirac page documents this from Arcam's manual, and no Denon document describes it for this model. The [licence comparison](../comparisons/which-dirac-license-to-buy.md) therefore has only one row for this model.

## Streaming

[HEOS](../systems/heos.md) and AirPlay 2 are built in (AV Nirvana, 2026-05-14), and Denon's product page carries a Spotify badge. No X2900H document in the evidence confirms the TIDAL Connect, Qobuz Connect or Roon Ready toggles that the X3900H manual shows; a retailer lists Roon Ready, unconfirmed by Denon. On HEOS, Spotify arrives by Spotify Connect and Apple Music by AirPlay 2 only ([streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md)). The internet-radio formats are not documented for this model; Radio Paradise FLAC is expected to need the external WiiM, as on the X3900H ([Radio Paradise](../systems/radio-paradise.md)).

## Measurements

No independent measurement exists. ASR has not reviewed the X2900H, and its database holds no X2800H or X2700H row that could serve as a proxy. The X3900H's SINAD of 102 does not transfer: different model, different price tier, and Denon's 2022 generation showed that DAC performance can move between models. See [measurements and audibility](../concepts/measurements-and-audibility.md).

## Known issues

- **Spec inconsistency on Denon's own page:** the headline says 7.2 channels while the specification table says 7.1 processing with two sub outputs.
- No owner reports or firmware problems are in the evidence; the model launched in May 2026. The X3900H reviewer's mandatory HEOS account at setup may apply to the same platform, but has not been reported for this model.

## Price and availability

$1,349 list and $1,349 on denon.com, in stock, new only (2026-11-08); the launch price was the same (AV Nirvana, 2026-05-14). A retailer describes it as replacing the AVR-X2800H, which launched at $1,199 (Strata-gee, 2022-09-26); Denon makes no successor statement. The [AVR-X3900H](denon-avr-x3900h.md) costs $500 more. Warranty is 3 years when bought from an authorised dealer (Denon limited warranty, 2024-12-12).
