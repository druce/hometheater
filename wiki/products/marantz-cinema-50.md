# Marantz CINEMA 50

**The Series 1 CINEMA 50, at $2,499 on sale, keeps the full Dirac stack and MultEQ-X support, but no full independent measurement or captured manual backs it.**

Ranked in: [Room correction & bass management](../criteria/room-correction.md) · [Music & two-channel performance](../criteria/music-performance.md) · [Streaming & multiroom](../criteria/streaming-and-multiroom.md) · [Amplification & pre-outs](../criteria/amplification-and-preouts.md) · [Connectivity](../criteria/connectivity.md) · [Usability, app & firmware support](../criteria/usability-and-support.md) · [Value, reliability & resale](../criteria/value-and-reliability.md).

## Verdict

Provisional. Its best case is a discounted route to the same correction options as the [CINEMA 50 Series 2](marantz-cinema-50-series-2.md) at $501 less: Dirac Room Correction, Bass Control and ART are all available, and the Windows MultEQ-X app lists this model where it does not list the Series 2. Its main problem is evidence. This round captured no Series 1 manual or spec sheet, so sub outputs, pre-outs, digital inputs and streaming protocols are unconfirmed, and the only bench data is a forum member's chart set without published numbers.

## Room correction

Marantz's compare tile lists Audyssey MultEQ XT32 for this model (CINEMA 50 Series 2 product page, 2026-11-08). The tile is the only source: no Series 1 spec sheet or manual is captured, which is why the [Audyssey MultEQ (XT, XT32, MultEQ-X)](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md) page leaves the tier open. The Windows MultEQ-X supported-model list names "CINEMA 50" (Microsoft Store, updated 2026-05-09), and the iOS MultEQ Editor app lists it too.

[Dirac Live (Room Correction, Bass Control, ART)](../systems/dirac-live.md) is fully available as paid licences: Room Correction at $259–$349, Bass Control and ART at $299 each, with the same bundles as the Series 2, including Room Correction plus Bass Control for $599 (Dirac grid and device page, 2026-11-08). Marantz's US Dirac page lists this model as compatible without mentioning Bass Control or ART; that page is stale (it omits every Series 2 model), and Dirac's grid governs. The [which-licence comparison](../comparisons/which-dirac-license-to-buy.md) works through the choice.

## Streaming

[HEOS (Denon, Marantz)](../systems/heos.md) is the platform per Marantz's tile text. AirPlay 2, the Connect services and Roon Ready are documented for the Series 2 but were not checked for this model; see [streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md).

- **[Radio Paradise](../systems/radio-paradise.md) FLAC:** internet-radio formats are unchecked for the Series 1, and HEOS has no Radio Paradise service on the models checked, so plan on a WiiM Pro or Pro Plus on a digital input. Whether this model has a coax input is not confirmed in the sources.
- **Spotify and Apple Music:** expected through Spotify Connect and AirPlay as on the Series 2, unconfirmed here.

## Measurements

No full independent review exists. Reading the numbers: [measurements and audibility](../concepts/measurements-and-audibility.md), [power ratings](../concepts/amplifier-power-ratings.md).

An ASR forum member measured a friend's unit, and ASR's reviewer promoted the set to the site's home page (ASR forum, 2023-01-02 to 01-03). The conditions were SINAD at 5 W into 4 ohms, front left and right, from optical and from RCA analog input, at 20, 22.4 and 40 kHz bandwidths; 8 ohms, 5 W, one channel, RCA in; and a 4-ohm multitone. Every value is inside chart images that were not captured, so there is no number to quote, no verdict and no row in ASR's database.

The nearest reviewed sibling scored SINAD 98 and was recommended (proxy: CINEMA 40, ASR, 2023-12-19, $3,500 at the time). ASR found weak filter attenuation, a 6 kHz jitter spur that worsened over HDMI, disappointing crosstalk, and no warm-up fault of the kind it measured on the Cinema 70s. Whether the CINEMA 50 shares the 40's amplifier is not stated anywhere in the sources.

## Known issues

- No measured problems and no owner reports for this model appear in the sources. The Cinema 70s warm-up fault did not show up on the Cinema 40, so it was not generation-wide, but the CINEMA 50 itself was not tested for it in any captured number.
- Firmware state unknown.

## Price and availability

$2,499 at marantz.com with a sale badge against a $2,800 list (2026-11-08); stock state was not captured. The original 2022 US launch price is unconfirmed. The model was announced in October 2022 and sold from November–December 2022 (round-1 verification: Phile-web, 2022-10-26; audiophile.no, 2022-11-15). Its successor, the [CINEMA 50 Series 2](marantz-cinema-50-series-2.md), was announced 2026-08-06 at $3,000, and both are on sale together. US warranty is 5 years (Marantz limited warranty page, captured 2026-11-08).
