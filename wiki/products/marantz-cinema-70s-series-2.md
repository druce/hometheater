# Marantz CINEMA 70s Series 2

**At $1,500 the CINEMA 70s Series 2 is the cheapest Marantz here, but MultEQ XT, no Dirac Bass Control and an unmeasured amplifier whose predecessor faulted at 4 ohms make it a weak fit.**

Ranked in: [Room correction & bass management](../criteria/room-correction.md) · [Music & two-channel performance](../criteria/music-performance.md) · [Streaming & multiroom](../criteria/streaming-and-multiroom.md) · [Amplification & pre-outs](../criteria/amplification-and-preouts.md) · [Connectivity](../criteria/connectivity.md) · [Usability, app & firmware support](../criteria/usability-and-support.md) · [Value, reliability & resale](../criteria/value-and-reliability.md).

## Verdict

Provisional. Its best case is a slim 5.1 receiver with the full HEOS and Connect streaming set at half the price of the CINEMA 50 Series 2, if your speakers are 6 ohms or higher and Audyssey or Dirac Room Correction alone will do. Its main problem is that both of your priorities hit a ceiling: the built-in Audyssey is the lower XT tier, Dirac cannot add Bass Control, and no one has measured the Series 2, so the Series 1 amplifier fault stays an open risk.

## Room correction

Audyssey MultEQ XT ships built in (info sheet p.2), the lower of the two tiers on the [Audyssey MultEQ (XT, XT32, MultEQ-X)](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md) page. The $19.99 iOS MultEQ Editor app lists this model; the Windows MultEQ-X list names only the Series 1 CINEMA 70S (App Store and Microsoft Store, 2026-11-08).

[Dirac Live (Room Correction, Bass Control, ART)](../systems/dirac-live.md) is a paid licence limited to Room Correction. Dirac's grid marks Bass Control and ART "Not supported" for this model, and the manual links the separate Dirac Live RC manual where the 50 Series 2 links the full one (Dirac grid, 2026-11-08; owner's manuals). Dirac shows a $259–$349 range without saying whether a Limited tier is sold here (open question #18). Without Bass Control, sub crossover, distance and level are set by hand after a Dirac run. Marantz's own US Dirac page omits every Series 2 model, so Dirac's grid is the current record. The [which-licence comparison](../comparisons/which-dirac-license-to-buy.md) costs the alternatives.

## Streaming

[HEOS (Denon, Marantz)](../systems/heos.md) is the platform, with AirPlay 2, Spotify Connect, TIDAL Connect, Qobuz Connect and Roon Ready listed and no Google Cast or native Apple Music (info sheet p.2; owner's manual network menu). See [streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md).

- **[Radio Paradise](../systems/radio-paradise.md) FLAC:** not from the receiver. Internet radio plays WMA, MP3 and AAC at 32–48 kHz only (owner's manual), so FLAC comes from a WiiM Pro or Pro Plus on the single coax input.
- **Spotify:** Spotify Connect.
- **Apple Music:** AirPlay 2 only.

FLAC and ALAC files play to 192 kHz from USB or a NAS (owner's manual), which does not help with radio.

## Measurements

No independent measurement of the Series 2 exists in the evidence. Reading the numbers: [measurements and audibility](../concepts/measurements-and-audibility.md), [power ratings](../concepts/amplifier-power-ratings.md).

The closest data is ASR's review of the Series 1 (proxy: CINEMA 70s, ASR, 2023-12-11). Its pre-out SINAD was 94 dB at 2 V over HDMI, with Toslink no better, and ASR did not recommend it. In the amplifier warm-up test, 5 W into each of two 4-ohm channels with ECO off, distortion rose about 10 dB after roughly 2 minutes and stayed there until a power cycle; it did not recur at 1 or 2 W. Higher-level 4-ohm sweeps tripped protection that also needed a power cycle. ASR noted that Marantz rates the amplifier no lower than 6 ohms.

The Series 2 press release credits a new DAC, streaming and connectivity and names no amplifier change, and both generations carry the same 50 W rating (HARMAN, 2026-08-06; Marantz store tiles, 2026-11-08). Whether the fault carries over is unknown (open question #26).

## Known issues

- **4-ohm warm-up distortion and protection latching:** measured on the Series 1 only, unmeasured on the Series 2. Relevant if your speakers need the 4-ohm setting.
- Low-latency Bluetooth LE Audio is promised for a future firmware update and is not confirmed shipped (HARMAN, 2026-08-06).
- No Series 2 firmware version or owner reports appear in the sources.

## Price and availability

$1,500 at marantz.com, no sale badge, in stock (2026-11-08), the same as the launch price; shipping began 2026-09-15 (HARMAN, 2026-08-06). The Series 1 CINEMA 70s sells alongside it at $999 against a $1,300 list (marantz.com, 2026-11-08). No successor is announced. US warranty is 5 years (Marantz limited warranty page, captured 2026-11-08).
