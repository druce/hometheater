# Denon AVR-X3900H

**The best-measured Denon candidate (ASR SINAD 102, recommended) at $1,849, with XT32 included and the full Dirac stack for sale, though one-sub Dirac adds $599 and HEOS cannot play Radio Paradise FLAC.**

Ranked in: [Room correction & bass management](../criteria/room-correction.md) · [Music & two-channel performance](../criteria/music-performance.md) · [Streaming & multiroom](../criteria/streaming-and-multiroom.md) · [Amplification & pre-outs](../criteria/amplification-and-preouts.md) · [Connectivity](../criteria/connectivity.md) · [Usability, app & firmware support](../criteria/usability-and-support.md) · [Value, reliability & resale](../criteria/value-and-reliability.md).

## Verdict

Provisional: for a 5.1 room with one sub, the X3900H is the safest all-round Denon. It pairs the strongest independent measurements in the Denon line below the flagship with every Dirac licence, Bass Control included, and leaves four amp channels and full pre-outs spare. The main problem is cost: Room Correction plus Bass Control brings the total to $2,448, while an Onkyo TX-RZ50 or Arcam AVA15 gets the same Dirac pair for $299 on top of the receiver. Radio Paradise FLAC has to come from the external streamer, as on every HEOS receiver.

## Room correction

[Audyssey MultEQ XT32](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md) is included. The $19.99 iOS MultEQ Editor app lists the X3900H; the Windows MultEQ-X store listing does not, and states that unlisted models are not compatible (both captured 2026-11-08), so MultEQ-X support is unconfirmed.

All three [Dirac Live](../systems/dirac-live.md) licences are available and paid (Dirac device page, 2026-11-08):

| Licence | Price |
|---|---|
| Room Correction, Limited (20–500 Hz) / Full | $259 / $349 |
| Bass Control | $299 |
| ART | $299 |
| Bundle: Room Correction + Bass Control | $599 |
| Bundle: all three | $799 |

For one sub, the useful purchase is Room Correction plus Bass Control: $599 as a bundle with Full bandwidth, or $558 with Limited. The [licence comparison](../comparisons/which-dirac-license-to-buy.md) works through the choice. Denon's own US Dirac page omits the X3900H; Dirac's grid, Denon's info sheet ("Full Dirac Live Suite") and the manual all include it, and Dirac is the licensor. Whether Denon supplies a Dirac microphone is unknown. Pure Direct and Direct bypass both Audyssey and Dirac (manual p.133), so corrected stereo music needs a mode such as Stereo; see [bass management and crossovers](../concepts/bass-management-and-crossovers.md).

## Streaming

[HEOS](../systems/heos.md) is built in, with separate network toggles for AirPlay, Spotify Connect, Roon Ready, TIDAL Connect and Qobuz Connect (manual p.244). Google Cast is not listed anywhere in the manual. Per [streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md), the three music sources arrive as follows:

- **Radio Paradise FLAC:** not through HEOS. Internet radio plays WMA, MP3 and AAC only (manual pp.285, 303), and Radio Paradise is not a HEOS service. The plan of record is a WiiM Pro or Pro Plus into one of the two coax inputs; see [Radio Paradise](../systems/radio-paradise.md).
- **Spotify:** Spotify Connect, free or Premium (manual p.118).
- **Apple Music:** AirPlay 2 from an Apple device only; there is no native app.

Zone 2 takes coax and optical as 2-channel PCM only, so the WiiM on coax can feed it, as can analog, USB, HEOS Music and the tuner (manual p.163). It has tone, level and a high-pass filter but no stated room correction or sub output, and keeps playing with the main zone off (pp.252, 270); [multiroom options](../comparisons/multiroom-options.md) weighs it against WiiM and Sonos.

**Control.** Denon splits control across two iOS apps: AVR Remote for power, volume, input and settings (2.0 stars on 1,456 ratings, last updated 2026-09-15) and HEOS for music (4.39 on 27,267); neither App Store listing claims Siri or Shortcuts support (2026-11-09). A browser control page covers zone, volume, source, sound mode and Quick Select (manual pp.154–156). Every network route needs Network Control at Always On, the default, which costs under 2 W in standby (p.241; info sheet). Four Quick Selects store input, sound mode and Dirac slot among other settings (pp.147–148) and can be recalled with the HEOS CLI v1.17 `play_quickselect` command, untested on this model. HDMI Control is off by default (p.191); once it is on, Power Off Control's default, All, puts the receiver in standby whenever the TV turns off, coax music included (p.192). Home Assistant's denonavr integration lists the X3800H but not the X3900H (2026-11-09). The routes are compared in [control and automation](../comparisons/control-and-automation.md).

## Measurements

Audio Science Review measured a SINAD of 102 dB and recommended the unit (ASR, X3900H review, 2026-09-17). The figure comes from the DAC test, taken at the pre-out with the amplifiers switched off. Same-method comparison points: X3800H 87, X4800H 95, X3600H 99, A1H 105 (ASR database). ASR found the best pre-out level near 1.5 V, which matters only if an external amplifier is added. It also noted low-level noise in the jitter test and wideband noise plus distortion that did not beat its $99 reference DAC.

The amplifier was tested from HDMI and analog inputs at 25 dB gain. ASR rated it very good for the class, found power levels unchanged from the previous generation and noise lower, and saw distortion take over from 1 to 2 W at higher frequencies. The watt figures sit in chart images that were not captured. What these numbers mean for audibility is on [measurements and audibility](../concepts/measurements-and-audibility.md).

## Known issues

- **Mandatory HEOS account (reviewer observation).** ASR's unit would not finish first-run setup without creating one; the manual ties the account only to Wi-Fi features (p.238). The review gives no firmware version.
- **Slow restart after an update (one reviewer).** Firmware updated through the HEOS app, then the unit rebooted later and took a long time to start (ASR, 2026-09-17).
- No repeated owner reports specific to this model are in the evidence; it launched in May 2026.

## Price and availability

$1,849 list and $1,849 on denon.com, in stock, new or refurbished (2026-11-08). The launch price was the same (AV Nirvana, 2026-05-14), so no discount has been seen. A retailer describes it as replacing the X3800H, which Denon still sells at $1,399 and does not call superseded. Warranty is 3 years when bought from an authorised dealer (Denon limited warranty, 2024-12-12).
