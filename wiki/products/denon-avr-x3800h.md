# Denon AVR-X3800H

**The 2022 X3800H is $450 cheaper than the X3900H at $1,399 and takes the same Dirac licences, but ASR measured its DAC at SINAD 87 and did not recommend it, and its full spec sheet is uncaptured.**

Ranked in: [Room correction & bass management](../criteria/room-correction.md) · [Music & two-channel performance](../criteria/music-performance.md) · [Streaming & multiroom](../criteria/streaming-and-multiroom.md) · [Amplification & pre-outs](../criteria/amplification-and-preouts.md) · [Connectivity](../criteria/connectivity.md) · [Usability, app & firmware support](../criteria/usability-and-support.md) · [Value, reliability & resale](../criteria/value-and-reliability.md).

## Verdict

Provisional: the X3800H's best case is the cheapest Denon route to Dirac Room Correction plus Bass Control, $1,998 all in against $2,448 for the X3900H at 2026-11-08 prices, with Windows MultEQ-X support confirmed where the X3900H's is not. Its main problem is the measured DAC regression, a 15 dB SINAD gap to its successor on the same test. Whether that gap is audible is argued on [measurements and audibility](../concepts/measurements-and-audibility.md); at a $450 difference the newer model is the default, and the X3800H is the pick only if that saving matters more than the measurement.

## Room correction

The [Audyssey MultEQ](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md) tier that ships is not confirmed in the sources read. The X3800H appears on both the iOS MultEQ Editor list and the Windows MultEQ-X list (App Store and Microsoft Store, captured 2026-11-08), which makes it the one Denon candidate with confirmed MultEQ-X support.

All three [Dirac Live](../systems/dirac-live.md) licences are available: Room Correction at $259–$349 (the Limited/Full split is not itemised on its device page), Bass Control $299, ART $299, with the same bundles as the X3900H, including Room Correction plus Bass Control for $599 (Dirac grid and device page, 2026-11-08). Denon's own Dirac page also lists it. For a one-sub room, the [licence comparison](../comparisons/which-dirac-license-to-buy.md) applies unchanged from the X3900H.

## Streaming

Denon lists the X3800H as powered by [HEOS](../systems/heos.md) (Denon category page, 2026-11-08). No X3800H document in the evidence lists its AirPlay, Connect or Roon support, so the streaming list above carries only HEOS. On the HEOS platform generally, Spotify arrives by Spotify Connect and Apple Music by AirPlay 2 ([streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md)). Its internet-radio format table is not captured; the X3900H's is lossy-only, so Radio Paradise FLAC is expected to need the external WiiM on a digital input here too ([Radio Paradise](../systems/radio-paradise.md)).

## Measurements

Audio Science Review measured a SINAD of 87 dB and did not recommend the unit (ASR, X3800H review, 2022-10-27; database row 10/26/22). The test used the Toslink input with the amplifiers shut off, measuring the pre-out. ASR blamed high distortion spikes and an optimum output level that had moved lower in voltage, where the X3700H (SINAD 101) had performed best at 2 V. ASR's two figures for the gap differ: about 10 dB in the review text against 14 dB between the database rows. Filter performance, linearity and IMD were also worse; dynamic range was unchanged. ASR reported that Denon's own DAC measurements matched its results within normal margin, so the regression is not a test artefact.

The amplifier fared better. ASR found it apparently the same design as the X3700H's, with a slightly weaker dashboard result and otherwise unchanged performance. ASR's X4800H review later attributed the generation's DAC regression to supply-chain issues; that is the reviewer's inference, not a Denon statement. The reviewer also allowed that subjective performance may be similar.

## Known issues

- **Heat (one reviewer).** The top front got quite hot while the internal fan did not run; ASR advised ample airflow (2022-10-27). This matters for cabinet placement in a small closed room.
- **Menu lock-up (one reviewer).** The Setup button stopped opening menus until a power cycle; firmware not stated.
- **HDMI board replaced (one owner).** A single AVS Forum post reports no HDMI picture fixed by replacing the digital board (2024-09-13). One post is not a pattern.
- Current firmware state is not documented in the evidence.

## Price and availability

Launch MSRP was $1,699 (Strata-gee, 2022-09-26; ASR, 2022). On 2026-11-08 denon.com showed $1,399 marked as reduced from $1,799, so the list price was raised at some point; no source says when. Stock state was not captured. The [AVR-X3900H](denon-avr-x3900h.md) is the newer model at $1,849, but Denon still sells the X3800H and does not call it superseded. Warranty is 3 years when bought from an authorised dealer (Denon limited warranty, 2024-12-12).
