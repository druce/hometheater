# Marantz CINEMA 50 Series 2

**The CINEMA 50 Series 2 offers the same XT32 and full Dirac options as the $1,849 Denon AVR-X3900H for $3,000, and no measurement yet shows what the extra $1,151 buys a 5.1 room.**

Ranked in: [Room correction & bass management](../criteria/room-correction.md) · [Music & two-channel performance](../criteria/music-performance.md) · [Streaming & multiroom](../criteria/streaming-and-multiroom.md) · [Amplification & pre-outs](../criteria/amplification-and-preouts.md) · [Connectivity](../criteria/connectivity.md) · [Usability, app & firmware support](../criteria/usability-and-support.md) · [Value, reliability & resale](../criteria/value-and-reliability.md).

## Verdict

Provisional. Its best case is the Marantz with no room-correction ceiling: XT32 in the box, Dirac Room Correction plus Bass Control available for $599, four independently adjustable sub outputs, two coax inputs and a 5-year warranty. Its main problem is price against what a one-sub 5.1 room uses. The nine amps, 11.4 processing, Atmos Channel Expander and IMAX Enhanced go largely idle here, the Denon X3900H offers the same correction stack and is measured (see [Denon AVR-X3900H](denon-avr-x3900h.md)), and this model has no independent measurement at all.

Manufacturer figures in Direct mode are 100 dB S/N and 0.005% distortion on the analog path, and 104 dB S/N for the D/A stage (owner's manual). Crossover and LFE low-pass ranges match the CINEMA 70s Series 2; see [bass management and crossovers](../concepts/bass-management-and-crossovers.md). A Center Bi-Amp mode, new and exclusive to this model, can use spare amp channels if your center speaker has bi-amp terminals (HARMAN, 2026-08-06).

## Room correction

Audyssey MultEQ XT32 ships built in (info sheet p.2), the higher tier on the [Audyssey MultEQ (XT, XT32, MultEQ-X)](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md) page. The $19.99 iOS MultEQ Editor app lists this model; the Windows MultEQ-X list does not (open question #5).

All three [Dirac Live (Room Correction, Bass Control, ART)](../systems/dirac-live.md) licences are available and all are paid; the manual says a licence must be bought from Dirac (Dirac grid, 2026-11-08; owner's manual). Room Correction is listed at $259–$349 without a stated Limited tier (open question #18), Bass Control and ART are $299 each, and the Room Correction plus Bass Control bundle is $599, down from $648 (Dirac device page, 2026-11-08). That bundle is the purchase that matters for one sub; the [which-licence comparison](../comparisons/which-dirac-license-to-buy.md) works through it. Marantz's page title reads "with Dolby Atmos, Dirac Live", but its own spec line says "(upgrade)", and Marantz's US Dirac page omits every Series 2 model.

## Streaming

[HEOS (Denon, Marantz)](../systems/heos.md) is the platform, with the same set as the 70s Series 2: AirPlay 2, Spotify Connect, TIDAL Connect, Qobuz Connect and Roon Ready, and no Google Cast or native Apple Music (info sheet p.2; owner's manual). See [streaming protocols](../systems/streaming-protocols.md).

- **[Radio Paradise](../systems/radio-paradise.md) FLAC:** not from the receiver. Internet radio plays WMA, MP3 and AAC at 32–48 kHz only (owner's manual), so FLAC comes from a WiiM Pro or Pro Plus on one of the two coax inputs.
- **Spotify:** Spotify Connect.
- **Apple Music:** AirPlay 2 only.

## Measurements

No independent measurement of the Series 2 exists in the evidence. Reading the numbers: [measurements and audibility](../concepts/measurements-and-audibility.md), [power ratings](../concepts/amplifier-power-ratings.md).

Two proxies exist, both weak. The Series 1 [CINEMA 50](marantz-cinema-50.md) carries the same 110 W, 9.4-channel rating but was measured only by an ASR forum member, with the values in images that were not captured (ASR forum, 2023-01). The sibling CINEMA 40 scored SINAD 98 and was recommended, with no warm-up fault (proxy: CINEMA 40, ASR, 2023-12-19). Series 2 changes the DAC, so neither proxy settles its DAC performance.

## Known issues

- Unmeasured, as above.
- Marantz says every Series 2 model has a new 32-bit eight-channel DAC, which it does not reconcile with this model's 11.4 processing channels (HARMAN, 2026-08-06; info sheet p.1). The info sheet's 11 speaker terminals against nine amps are also unexplained.
- Low-latency Bluetooth LE Audio is promised for a future firmware update and is not confirmed shipped (HARMAN, 2026-08-06).
- No firmware version or owner reports for the Series 2 appear in the sources.

## Price and availability

$3,000 at marantz.com, no sale badge, in stock, black only (2026-11-08), the same as the launch price; announced 2026-08-06 and available from 2026-08-12 (HARMAN). The Series 1 CINEMA 50 sells alongside it at $2,499 against a $2,800 list (marantz.com, 2026-11-08). No successor is announced. US warranty is 5 years (Marantz limited warranty page, captured 2026-11-08).
