# Recommendation

**Buy the Denon AVR-X3900H, the Dirac Room Correction plus Bass Control bundle, and a WiiM Pro on its coax input: $2,617 at 2026-11-08 prices, with no outcome that depends on speakers not yet chosen.**

## Recommendation

| Item | Price (2026-11-08) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [Denon AVR-X3900H](../products/denon-avr-x3900h.md) | $1,849, denon.com, in stock | Best-measured candidate (ASR SINAD 102, recommended); four independent sub outputs; XT32 included as a baseline |
| [Dirac](../systems/dirac-live.md) Room Correction (Full) + Bass Control bundle | $599, dirac.com | Bass Control computes crossover, sub distance and level that Room Correction alone leaves to hand ([licence comparison](../comparisons/which-dirac-license-to-buy.md)) |
| [WiiM Pro](../systems/wiim.md) on coax | $169, wiimhome.com | Radio Paradise FLAC, which HEOS cannot play; the Pro Plus's extra $50 buys an analog stage this chain does not use ([comparison](../comparisons/radio-paradise-flac-native-vs-external-streamer.md)) |
| **Total** | **$2,617** | |

Spotify arrives by Spotify Connect on the receiver and Apple Music by AirPlay 2 from an Apple device, both documented in the Denon manual. The Dirac bundle can wait: XT32 runs from day one, and buying the bundle later costs nothing extra while Dirac's struck-through list prices stay where they are. A Dirac microphone may not be supplied with a Denon upgrade (open question #19), so a calibrated USB microphone may be an extra cost not counted above.

The [Onkyo TX-RZ50](../products/onkyo-tx-rz50.md) is the conditional alternative at $1,868. It becomes the better buy if three things hold together: the chosen speakers are nominal 8 Ω without deep impedance dips, a backorder of months is acceptable, and a 2020 platform with a 2027 successor in view is acceptable for resale. ASR saw no limiting at 8 Ω, but no one has measured how close five channels at small-room levels come to the threshold (#14), and only an AC-cord pull clears it. The $749 saving is real; the risk is unpriced.

The two future stereo rooms take a [WiiM Amp Ultra](../products/wiim-amp-ultra.md) each ($549), with the kitchen and bathroom speakers chosen later; the WiiM Pro in the main room joins the same app.

## What would change it

- **The X3900H's price.** A rise of more than $450 makes the [AVR-X3800H](../products/denon-avr-x3800h.md) worth its weaker DAC measurement (SINAD 87); a sale below roughly $1,400 removes most of the RZ50's advantage.
- **Speaker impedance (#4).** Speakers at 8 Ω nominal with benign minimums reopen the RZ50; 4 Ω or 6 Ω speakers close it.
- **An RZ50 sweep on current firmware (#6)** showing no limiter would make it the value pick outright.
- **Radio Paradise on the WiiM Pro (#10, #23).** If a device test shows the WiiM app delivers a lossy stream and the `/flac` URL cannot be played, casting the RP app's FLAC to the Pro over Google Cast, which owners report as lossless, is the next route, and only its failure reopens the [NAD T 758 V3i](../products/nad-t-758-v3i.md).
- **A measurement of Bass Control against a hand-set single sub.** A small difference makes the $599 bundle optional and cuts the X3900H's total to $2,018 with XT32 alone or $2,367 with Room Correction (Full) only.
