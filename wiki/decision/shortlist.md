# Shortlist

**Two receivers survive the seven criteria: the Denon AVR-X3900H, first on five of them, and the Onkyo TX-RZ50, the only way to the same Dirac target for $749 less.**

The shortlist follows the hub rankings in the [decision matrix](../reference/matrix.md) and the owner's priorities on [my system](../start-here/my-system.md): room correction and music first, one sub, a small closed room, Radio Paradise FLAC through a WiiM Pro on coax. "To target" means receiver plus Dirac Room Correction and Bass Control plus a $169 WiiM Pro, at 2026-11-08 prices.

## Shortlist

| | [Denon AVR-X3900H](../products/denon-avr-x3900h.md) | [Onkyo TX-RZ50](../products/onkyo-tx-rz50.md) |
|---|---|---|
| Cost to target | $2,617: $1,849 + $599 Dirac bundle + $169 | $1,868: $1,399.99 + $299 Bass Control + $169 |
| Room correction | XT32 included; Dirac RC, Bass Control and ART all for sale | Full-bandwidth Dirac RC and mic included; Bass Control $299; no ART, no Audyssey |
| Measured | ASR SINAD 102, recommended (2026-09-17) | ASR SINAD 77 (table, conditions unstated); latched 4 Ω power limiter (2022-02-12) |
| Sub outputs | 4, independent | 2; independence not stated |
| Stock and warranty | In stock; 3 years via an authorised dealer | Backorder, months per a 2026-10-15 buyer; 3 years |
| Platform age | Launched May 2026 | December 2020; successors teased for 2027 |

**The X3900H** is the choice that holds whatever speakers are bought. It ranks first on [room correction](../criteria/room-correction.md), [music](../criteria/music-performance.md), [streaming](../criteria/streaming-and-multiroom.md), [amplification](../criteria/amplification-and-preouts.md) and [value](../criteria/value-and-reliability.md), and second on usability behind the CINEMA 50 Series 2. Its cost is the $599 Dirac bundle that Onkyo largely includes.

**The RZ50** is on the list for price alone. Its limiter was measured at 4 Ω and not seen at 8 Ω, so whether it matters turns on speaker impedance, which is still open (#4), and on how often limiting triggers at small-room levels, which nobody has measured (#14).

## Eliminated and why

- [Denon AVR-X3800H](../products/denon-avr-x3800h.md): $450 below the X3900H to target on the same Dirac licences, but ASR measured its DAC at SINAD 87 and did not recommend it. It is the fallback if the X3900H's price rises before purchase.
- [Marantz CINEMA 50 Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-50-series-2.md): the X3900H's correction stack for $1,151 more; the 5-year warranty is the one documented difference, and it is unmeasured.
- [Marantz CINEMA 50](../products/marantz-cinema-50.md): $3,267 to target, no full measurement, XT32 resting on a single compare tile.
- [Arcam AVA15](../products/arcam-ava15.md): $4,967.95 to target with no measurement to show what $2,351 over the X3900H buys; US stock unconfirmed (#30).
- [Anthem MRX 540 8K](../products/anthem-mrx-540-8k.md): ARC Genesis is included, but receiver and ARC are both unmeasured, distances are entered by hand and the two sub outputs are paralleled.
- [Denon AVR-X2900H](../products/denon-avr-x2900h.md) and [Marantz CINEMA 70s Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-70s-series-2.md): Dirac Bass Control is not supported, so the sub is integrated by hand, which removes the reason to buy Dirac on them.
- [NAD T 758 V3i](../products/nad-t-758-v3i.md): the only native Radio Paradise FLAC path, but no Bass Control, no V3i measurement and a (proxy: T 758 V3) DAC SINAD near 55; a $169 streamer replaces what it adds ([comparison](../comparisons/radio-paradise-flac-native-vs-external-streamer.md)).
- [Yamaha RX-A4A](../products/yamaha-rx-a4a.md): no Dirac at all, YPAO's low-frequency correction is set by hand, and the unit is unmeasured.

The [WiiM Amp Ultra](../products/wiim-amp-ultra.md) is not a 5.1 candidate. It stays the plan for the two future stereo rooms.
