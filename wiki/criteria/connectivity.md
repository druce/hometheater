# Criterion 5: Connectivity

**Connectivity here reduces to a digital input for the WiiM and HDMI with eARC: the Arcam AVA15 and Yamaha RX-A4A document both best, and the NAD's 4K-only, ARC-only HDMI is the one real deficit.**

## Why this matters here

Two connections carry this room ([my system](../start-here/my-system.md)). The first is the digital input for the external streamer: a WiiM Pro or Pro Plus sends Radio Paradise over coax or optical, and the Pro Plus outputs up to 24/192 (WiiM pages, 2026-11-08). Radio Paradise's FLAC is 16/44.1 by owner reports, which sits inside every input limit any maker states, so one coax input is enough. A second digital input matters only if a TV, CD transport or second streamer also needs one. Most makers state no maximum input rate (open question #12); only Yamaha and Arcam do, and ASR fed the Onkyo TX-RZ50 192 kHz S/PDIF in its review.

The second is HDMI. The TV, consoles and other sources are still unknown, so HDMI is judged on what is cheap to need later: eARC on the TV output, enough inputs, and current HDMI 2.1 signals. Every candidate except the NAD passes 8K or 4K/120 on at least three inputs, so HDMI separates only the NAD and the two models that pass 8K on half their inputs.

## What good looks like

| Level | Threshold for this room | Cheapest candidate that reaches it (2026-11-08) |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum | One coax input for the WiiM and an HDMI output with eARC | Denon AVR-X2900H, $1,349: 1 coax, 2 optical, eARC on one of 2 outputs |
| Good | Two coax inputs and six or more HDMI inputs, all passing 8K | Denon AVR-X3900H, $1,849: 2 coax, 2 optical, 6 inputs all 8K |
| Best | The maker states a 192 kHz coax limit, with seven HDMI 2.1 inputs and eARC | Yamaha RX-A4A, $1,599.95 (audiolab.com): coax to 192 kHz, 7 in / 3 out at 40 Gbps |

## How the candidates rank

### Best for this criterion

1. [Arcam AVA15](../products/arcam-ava15.md): two coax inputs to 192 kHz and two optical to 96 kHz, both stated by Arcam, plus 7 in / 2 out HDMI 2.1a with eARC, VRR and ALLM (spec sheet v1.2).
2. [Yamaha RX-A4A](../products/yamaha-rx-a4a.md): one coax to 192 kHz and two optical to 96 kHz, stated in the user guide, plus 7 in / 3 out HDMI 2.1 with eARC and 8K60/4K120 pass-through at 40 Gbps.

### Good

3. [Denon AVR-X3900H](../products/denon-avr-x3900h.md): two coax and two optical with no stated rate; 6 HDMI inputs, all 8K, with TV 1 (eARC), TV 2 and Zone 2 outputs; the rear USB port supplies 5 V/1.5 A, enough to power a streamer.
4. [Marantz CINEMA 50 Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-50-series-2.md): the same two coax and two optical, rate unstated; 6 inputs all 8K/60 and 4K/120, two main outputs with eARC plus a Zone 2 HDMI output.
5. [Anthem MRX 540 8K](../products/anthem-mrx-540-8k.md): the most digital inputs here, two coax and three optical, rate unstated; 7 in / 2 out HDMI 2.1 with eARC and no Zone 2.
6. [Onkyo TX-RZ50](../products/onkyo-tx-rz50.md): one coax and one optical, rate unstated; 7 HDMI inputs, but the front one is 4K/60 only, and 2 outputs; a 14 Oct 2021 firmware fixed eARC with Sony TVs.
7. [Onkyo TX-RZ30](../products/onkyo-tx-rz30.md): one coax and one optical, both stated to 96 kHz/24-bit PCM (manual p.182), enough for Radio Paradise at 44.1 kHz; 6 HDMI inputs, 3 at full rate, one fewer than the RZ50.

### Weaker

8. [Denon AVR-X2900H](../products/denon-avr-x2900h.md): one coax, two optical; only 3 of its 6 HDMI inputs pass 8K, with 2 outputs.
9. [Marantz CINEMA 70s Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-70s-series-2.md): one coax, one optical; 3 of 6 inputs pass 8K/60 or 4K/120, and there is a single HDMI output and no Zone 2 HDMI.
10. [NAD T 758 V3i](../products/nad-t-758-v3i.md): HDMI is 3 in / 1 out at 4K/60 with HDCP 2.2 and ARC, with no HDMI 2.1 and no eARC listed; its digital input count is unstated, and BluOS makes a streamer input less necessary here.

### Not enough evidence to rank

- [Denon AVR-X3800H](../products/denon-avr-x3800h.md): 6 HDMI inputs at 8K and 3 outputs (one eARC) per Denon's comparison block, but its digital audio inputs are uncaptured (#21). Its info sheet would place it.
- [Marantz CINEMA 50](../products/marantz-cinema-50.md): 6 inputs at 8K and 2 outputs (one eARC) per Marantz's tile; digital inputs, including whether a coax exists, are uncaptured. A Series 1 spec sheet would place it.

## Open questions

From [open questions](../reference/open-questions.md):

- **#12** Maximum coax and optical sample rates on the X3900H, CINEMA Series 2, RZ50 and MRX 540. It does not bind a 44.1 kHz Radio Paradise stream, but it would separate the Good tier from the Best.
- **#21** X3800H digital inputs and X2900H Zone 2 HDMI.
- Not yet registered: the NAD T 758 V3i's coax and optical count. Anthem's 2022 series sheet claims three HDMI outputs with eARC for the line, while the 540's own page lists two.
- Owner: TV model and whether it has eARC, number of HDMI sources, and any vinyl, which decide how much HDMI and analog inputs matter at all.
