# Criterion 3: Streaming & multiroom

**The Denon AVR-X3900H and the Marantz CINEMA Series 2 models document the fullest streaming set for this room; only the NAD plays Radio Paradise natively, and no receiver shares an app with WiiM rooms.**

## Why this matters here

With a WiiM Pro or Pro Plus on a coax input carrying Radio Paradise FLAC ([my system](../start-here/my-system.md), owner decision 2026-11-08), the receiver's own platform is judged on three things: Spotify, Apple Music, and how it fits the four future rooms.

Spotify separates nobody, since every candidate with a documented protocol list carries Spotify Connect or a built-in Spotify client. Apple Music does not separate them either: none of the eleven receivers has a native Apple Music client, so Apple Music arrives by AirPlay from an iPhone or Mac on all of them, and the version is in doubt only where the maker leaves it unstated (Anthem, Arcam). WiiM's FAQ puts Apple Music over AirPlay at 44.1 kHz/16-bit, a vendor figure with no Apple source behind it (open question #24).

Multiroom is the real separator, and it works against the receiver. The main-room WiiM already puts WiiM Home in the house, and the stereo-room candidate, the WiiM Amp Ultra, runs the same app. Any receiver platform (HEOS, MusicCast, BluOS, Radia) is then a second app unless the whole house is built on it. The bridges between platforms are thin: WiiM network streams can join Google Cast or Alexa groups, but physical inputs are shared only inside an all-WiiM group (Amp Ultra product page), and the Amp Ultra has no AirPlay 2, so an iPhone cannot group it with an AirPlay 2 receiver. Whether a receiver's own Cast implementation joins a Cast group with WiiM players is untested for every candidate.

## What good looks like

| Level | Threshold for this room | Cheapest candidate that reaches it (2026-11-08) |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum | Spotify and AirPlay 2 documented on the receiver, plus a coax or optical input for the WiiM | Denon AVR-X2900H, $1,349: HEOS with AirPlay 2 (AV Nirvana, 2026-05-14), Spotify badge, one coax |
| Good | Adds TIDAL Connect, Qobuz Connect and Roon Ready, on a multiroom platform with stated group limits | Marantz CINEMA 70s Series 2, $1,500: full HEOS set per its info sheet; HEOS systems up to 32 products, synchronized groups up to 16 (X3900H manual p.111) |
| Best | Native lossless Radio Paradise and native Apple Music, in one app shared with the other four rooms | None confirmed. The NAD T 758 V3i ($2,299) has Radio Paradise on BluOS; Apple Music on it is unconfirmed (#25) |

## How the candidates rank

Ranked on the receiver's own platform, with Radio Paradise assumed to come from the external WiiM.

### Best for this criterion

1. [Denon AVR-X3900H](../products/denon-avr-x3900h.md): the manual documents AirPlay 2 plus separate toggles for Spotify, TIDAL and Qobuz Connect and Roon Ready; against it, there is no Google Cast, HEOS internet radio is WMA/MP3/AAC only, and ASR's unit required a HEOS account to finish setup (2026-09-17).
2. [Marantz CINEMA 50 Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-50-series-2.md): the same HEOS protocol set per its info sheet and manual, with the same lossy-only internet radio and no Cast; HEOS also groups with Denon Home speakers for the kitchen and bathroom.
3. [Marantz CINEMA 70s Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-70s-series-2.md): streaming identical to the 50 Series 2 at half the price ($1,500, 2026-11-08).

### Good

4. [Arcam AVA15](../products/arcam-ava15.md): the widest protocol list here (Google Cast, Spotify, TIDAL and Qobuz Connect, Roon Ready), but AirPlay's version is unstated and the new Radia app has no multiroom platform comparable to HEOS or BluOS.
5. [NAD T 758 V3i](../products/nad-t-758-v3i.md): the only receiver with Radio Paradise built in (BluOS, MQA unfolded in the receiver) and AirPlay 2 on its data sheet, but Spotify Connect and native Apple Music are not confirmed for this model (#25), and BluOS is a second app beside WiiM.
6. [Denon AVR-X2900H](../products/denon-avr-x2900h.md): HEOS and AirPlay 2 confirmed; Denon confirms none of the TIDAL Connect, Qobuz Connect or Roon Ready toggles that the X3900H manual shows.
7. [Yamaha RX-A4A](../products/yamaha-rx-a4a.md): AirPlay 2 and Spotify Connect, with TIDAL and Qobuz in the app rather than as Connect; linked MusicCast rooms are resampled to 48 kHz, and AirPlay volume is capped at −20 dB by default.
8. [Onkyo TX-RZ50](../products/onkyo-tx-rz50.md): Google Cast, AirPlay 2, DTS Play-Fi and Works with Sonos, but Onkyo does not say whether Spotify and TIDAL are the Connect protocols, and "Roon Tested" is not Roon Ready.

### Weaker

9. [Anthem MRX 540 8K](../products/anthem-mrx-540-8k.md): Spotify Connect and Cast, while the 2022 sheet says AirPlay 2 and the current page says AirPlay; there is no TIDAL or Qobuz Connect, no internet radio and no multiroom platform, and the promised Roon support no longer appears.

### Not enough evidence to rank

- [Denon AVR-X3800H](../products/denon-avr-x3800h.md): only HEOS is documented. An X3800H manual listing its AirPlay and Connect toggles would place it beside the X3900H (#21).
- [Marantz CINEMA 50](../products/marantz-cinema-50.md): only "Powered by HEOS" is captured, and whether it has a coax input for the WiiM is unconfirmed. A Series 1 info sheet would place it.

### Stereo rooms (not a 5.1 candidate)

- [WiiM Amp Ultra](../products/wiim-amp-ultra.md): shares WiiM Home with the main-room WiiM and has Spotify Connect and an owner-reported lossless Radio Paradise service (#10), but **no AirPlay 2 and no native Apple Music**, so Apple Music reaches it only by Bluetooth (44.1/16 per WiiM), Google Cast from a supporting app, or cable. The WiiM Pro, Pro Plus, Mini and older Amp keep AirPlay 2 (WiiM pages, 2026-11-08).

## Open questions

From [open questions](../reference/open-questions.md):

- **#25** Whether BluOS's native Apple Music (announced 2026-10-20) reaches the T 758 V3i. A yes would move the NAD into the top tier on paper.
- **#10, #23** Whether the WiiM app's Radio Paradise service, or RP's `/flac` URL on a WiiM Pro or Pro Plus, delivers 16/44.1 FLAC out the coax. Every ranking above assumes it does.
- **#24** Apple's own figure for the AirPlay 2 format ceiling.
- **#13** What a Bluesound NODE sends out its coax for Radio Paradise, which decides whether a NODE can replace the WiiM.
- **#9, #22, #16** Whether HEOS (via Roon, AirPlay or the CLI `play_stream` command) or Yamaha's airable directory can play RP FLAC natively; none is device-tested.
- **#21, #28** The X3800H's protocol list, and whether the MRX 540 is now Roon Ready.
- Not yet registered: whether any candidate's Google Cast joins a multiroom Cast group with WiiM players, which would let one app (Google Home) span the house.
