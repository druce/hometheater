# Criterion 6: Usability, app & firmware support

**The Marantz CINEMA 50 Series 2 and Denon AVR-X3900H ask the least calibration work, with on-receiver Audyssey plus a Bass Control path; Marantz adds a 5-year warranty, and only Onkyo has a documented firmware record.**

## Why this matters here

Room correction ranks first in [my system](../start-here/my-system.md), and correction helps only if it is run, and run again. The sub is not bought yet, so the first calibration will be redone at least once when the sub arrives and again whenever it moves. The effort per run differs by system:

- **Audyssey** runs from the receiver with the supplied microphone at up to eight positions and sets distances, levels and the sub crossover itself ([Audyssey](../systems/audyssey-multeq.md)). Editable targets need the $19.99 iOS Editor app or Windows MultEQ-X.
- **Dirac** runs from Windows or Mac software or a phone app. With Bass Control it computes crossover, sub distance and sub level; with Room Correction alone those are set by hand (Arcam manual p.40, via [Dirac Live](../systems/dirac-live.md)). Whether Denon and Marantz supply a Dirac microphone is unknown (#19).
- **ARC Genesis** needs a Windows or Mac computer, and distances are measured and entered by hand ([ARC Genesis](../systems/arc-genesis.md)).
- **YPAO** runs from the receiver, about 15 minutes for eight positions, but its Low Frequency mode leaves Q and gain for the bass to be set by hand ([YPAO](../systems/ypao.md)).

Firmware and support decide whether that setup keeps working over the receiver's life. The evidence is thin: Onkyo's TX-RZ50 is the only receiver with dated firmware notes in the sources, and the 2026 models have no track record yet. Warranty is 3 years on Denon AVR-X models and Onkyo RZ receivers, and 5 years on Marantz CINEMA models, each from an authorised dealer (Denon warranty, 2024-12-12; Marantz and Onkyo warranty pages, captured 2026-11-08); no term is in the evidence for Yamaha, Anthem, NAD, Arcam or WiiM.

## What good looks like

| Level | Threshold for this room | Cheapest candidate that reaches it (2026-11-08) |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum | Automatic calibration from the receiver that sets distances, levels and sub crossover with a supplied mic, and a 3-year warranty | Denon AVR-X2900H, $1,349 (Audyssey MultEQ XT) |
| Good | Adds a Dirac route that computes sub alignment (Bass Control), with the mic included | Onkyo TX-RZ50, $1,399.99 + $299 Bass Control (backordered) |
| Best | Bass Control path plus on-receiver XT32 as a fallback and a 5-year warranty | Marantz CINEMA 50, $2,499 on sale + $599 Dirac bundle |

## How the candidates rank

### Best for this criterion

1. [Marantz CINEMA 50 Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-50-series-2.md): XT32 on the receiver, Dirac Room Correction plus Bass Control for $599, independent level and distance on four sub outputs, and a 5-year warranty; it has shipped only since 2026-08-12, so there is no firmware record, and Bluetooth LE Audio is promised but unshipped.
2. [Denon AVR-X3900H](../products/denon-avr-x3900h.md): the same correction stack with a Setup Assistant, web interface and USB settings backup, under a 3-year warranty; ASR's unit would not finish setup without a HEOS account and restarted slowly after a firmware update (2026-09-17, one unit).

### Good

3. [Marantz CINEMA 50](../products/marantz-cinema-50.md): XT32 per Marantz's tile, the full Dirac stack, Windows MultEQ-X support and a 5-year warranty; no manual is captured and firmware state is unknown.
4. [Onkyo TX-RZ50](../products/onkyo-tx-rz50.md): Dirac and a mic included, and dated firmware releases through 3 Mar 2026 fixed a Bass Control crossover error (1 Jul 2025), wrong Dirac filters on some layouts and silent iPhone Bluetooth (both 12 Mar 2025); no release addresses the 4-ohm limiter, which clears only with an AC-cord pull and shows no indicator.
5. [Denon AVR-X3800H](../products/denon-avr-x3800h.md): the one Denon with Windows MultEQ-X confirmed and a Bass Control path, under a 3-year warranty; one reviewer saw the Setup menu lock until a power cycle and noted heat with the fan idle (ASR, 2022-10-27).
6. [Marantz CINEMA 70s Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-70s-series-2.md): automatic Audyssey XT and a 5-year warranty, but Dirac cannot add Bass Control, so the sub is set by hand after any Dirac run.
7. [Denon AVR-X2900H](../products/denon-avr-x2900h.md): automatic Audyssey XT and a 3-year warranty; as with the 70s Series 2, Dirac leaves the sub to manual setup.

### Weaker

8. [Yamaha RX-A4A](../products/yamaha-rx-a4a.md): YPAO runs on the receiver but leaves bass Q and gain to the user, and defaults need undoing (YPAO Volume re-enables after every run, Compressed Music Enhancer is on for network inputs); Memory Guard and USB backup help, while firmware history and warranty are not in the evidence.
9. [Anthem MRX 540 8K](../products/anthem-mrx-540-8k.md): ARC Genesis needs a computer and hand-entered distances, Anthem's app was in public beta on the 2022 sheet, and on the (proxy: MRX 1140) the front panel missed a firmware update that the web interface found (ASR, 2025-11-04); warranty not captured.
10. [NAD T 758 V3i](../products/nad-t-758-v3i.md): Dirac without Bass Control (inferred) leaves sub distance, level and crossover to the user, and the (proxy: T 758 V3) menus were text-only with a four-way control (ASR, 2019-09-07); warranty not in the evidence.

### Not enough evidence to rank

- [Arcam AVA15](../products/arcam-ava15.md): Dirac and a mic are included and Bass Control computes, then locks, the sub values (manual p.40), but the Radia app and AVA line launched in 2026 with no owner reports, no firmware notes and no warranty term in the evidence. Owner reports after a few firmware releases would place it.

### Stereo rooms (not a 5.1 candidate)

- [WiiM Amp Ultra](../products/wiim-amp-ultra.md): RoomFit is free in the WiiM Home app and uses the phone's mic (no mic in the box), and firmware updates itself in standby unless delayed; the ASR reviewer's app failed to add the unit on the first try, there is no power button, and the security-support end date reads 2026 or 2029 depending on the source (#29).
