# Criterion 7: Value, reliability & resale

**The Denon AVR-X3900H at $2,617 with Dirac Room Correction, Bass Control and a WiiM Pro is the reference; only the backordered Onkyo TX-RZ50 reaches the same target for less, at $1,868.**

## Why this matters here

The budget is open, so each step up is priced against what it buys ([my system](../start-here/my-system.md)). The target used here is the one the [Dirac Live](../systems/dirac-live.md) page argues for a one-sub room, Room Correction plus Bass Control, plus a $169 WiiM Pro on coax for Radio Paradise FLAC ([WiiM](../systems/wiim.md); the Pro Plus adds $50 for an analog stage this system would not use). Whether Denon and Marantz include a Dirac microphone is unknown (#19), which could add to their totals. Licences do not obviously travel with the receiver: MultEQ-X licences are per user and per serial number and non-transferable (Microsoft Store listing), and Dirac's transfer terms are not in the evidence.

Reliability evidence is weak and mostly anecdote. The AVS Forum thread on HDMI repair (root post 2023-05-12) collects owner reports, labelled `[ANECDOTE]`: an X4300H HDMI port failing at about 4.5 years, a 9-month wait for a Denon board, one owner's belief that Denon makes boards only through the warranty period, Onkyo and Sony HDMI failures, and Yamaha supplying a board for an RX-A2070 about five years old. The only candidate-model report is one X3800H digital-board replacement (2024-09-13). The thread title selects for failures and no failure-rate data exists (#32). The measured reliability-relevant fault is the RZ50's latching 4-ohm limiter (ASR, 2022-02-12).

Ownership bears on long-term support. HARMAN, a Samsung Electronics subsidiary, completed its purchase of Sound United, which owns Denon, Marantz and HEOS, on 2025-09-23 and runs it as a standalone unit, with no stated roadmap commitment (HARMAN release). Onkyo belongs to Premium Audio Company, which Gentex acquired with VOXX on 2025-04-01; Gentex's statements speak of profitability and make no AVR commitment (VOXX 8-K exhibit). Arcam's HARMAN ownership rests on search summaries only.

## How the candidates rank

### Best for this criterion

1. [Denon AVR-X3900H](../products/denon-avr-x3900h.md): $2,617 to target on the best-measured candidate (ASR SINAD 102, recommended, 2026-09-17), in stock, 3-year warranty; its May 2026 launch price has not moved.
2. [Onkyo TX-RZ50](../products/onkyo-tx-rz50.md): $749 less to the same Dirac target, against a backorder a 2026-10-15 buyer describes as months long, the measured 4-ohm limiter, a 2020 platform whose successors Onkyo expects in 2027 (CES 2026 release), and Gentex ownership.

### Good

3. [Denon AVR-X3800H](../products/denon-avr-x3800h.md): $450 below the X3900H to target, but ASR measured its DAC at SINAD 87 and did not recommend it, and denon.com now shows $1,399 against a raised $1,799 list.
4. [Marantz CINEMA 50](../products/marantz-cinema-50.md): $3,267 to target with a 5-year warranty, on sale against a $2,800 list beside its successor; no full measurement and no captured manual.

### Weaker

5. [Yamaha RX-A4A](../products/yamaha-rx-a4a.md): the lowest total here at $1,768.95, but it cannot reach Dirac at all, is unmeasured, and retail listings run $1,399.95–$1,599.95 against a $1,299.95 launch MSRP (#29).
6. [Denon AVR-X2900H](../products/denon-avr-x2900h.md): $1,867 buys Room Correction without Bass Control, the same money as the RZ50's full target; unmeasured.
7. [Marantz CINEMA 70s Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-70s-series-2.md): $1,928–$2,018 for Room Correction only, with a 5-year warranty and an unmeasured amplifier whose predecessor faulted at 4 ohms.
8. [Anthem MRX 540 8K](../products/anthem-mrx-540-8k.md): $2,068.99 with ARC Genesis included and nothing to buy later, but both the receiver and ARC are unmeasured and the warranty term is uncaptured.
9. [Marantz CINEMA 50 Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-50-series-2.md): $1,151 more than the X3900H for the same correction stack; the 5-year warranty is the one documented difference, and it is unmeasured.
10. [NAD T 758 V3i](../products/nad-t-758-v3i.md): $2,299 with Radio Paradise built in and no streamer needed, but no Bass Control, no V3i measurement, and a (proxy: T 758 V3) DAC SINAD of about 55 (ASR, 2019).
11. [Arcam AVA15](../products/arcam-ava15.md): $4,967.95 to target, $2,351 above the X3900H with no measurement to show what that buys, US stock unconfirmed (#30) and no warranty term in the evidence.

### Stereo rooms (not a 5.1 candidate)

- [WiiM Amp Ultra](../products/wiim-amp-ultra.md): $549 on wiimhome.com ($529 when ASR reviewed and recommended it, 2025-12-29), $1,098 for two rooms with RoomFit free; warranty term and security-support horizon are unresolved (#29).

- **#6, #14** Whether firmware fixes the Onkyo limiter and how often it triggers; a yes would leave the RZ50 at the top on cost alone.
