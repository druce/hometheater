# Criterion 4: Amplification & pre-outs

**For 5.1 in a small room the rated watts barely separate the candidates; the Denon AVR-X3900H wins on a measured amplifier, full 11.4 pre-outs and four independent sub outputs, and the Onkyo TX-RZ50's latched 4-ohm limiter puts it near the bottom.**

## Why this matters here

The room in [my system](../start-here/my-system.md) is small and closed, and 5.1 uses five amplifier channels, so every candidate has at least the channels it needs and power is unlikely to be the limit with typical speakers. The ratings do not show that directly. Under the FTC rule a headline rating only has to hold for the two front channels, into 8 ohms, 20 Hz–20 kHz, and makers state it at THD levels from 0.06% (Yamaha) to 1% (Anthem) ([amplifier power ratings](../concepts/amplifier-power-ratings.md)). Measured five-channel output on siblings falls well short: 89 W from an Anthem MRX 740 rated 140 W, and 81 W from a Yamaha RX-A6A rated 150 W (Audioholics, 2022 and 2021). No candidate's five-channel output has been measured.

What separates candidates is behaviour under load, and that depends on speakers not yet chosen (open question #4). A receiver that limits or latches into protection at 4 ohms is fine with an easy 8-ohm load and a problem with a speaker that dips low. Two other requirements look ahead. Full pre-outs let an external amplifier take over the mains later without replacing the receiver. A second, independently adjustable sub output keeps a second sub open, which [my system](../start-here/my-system.md) lists as a nice-to-have; with one sub today, same-signal outputs cost nothing. Two settings reduce power on several candidates: ECO modes lower the supply rail on Denon and Yamaha, and low-impedance selectors are reported by reviewers to cut power, so the default impedance setting suits 6-ohm-or-higher speakers.

## What good looks like

| Level | Threshold for this room | Cheapest candidate that reaches it (2026-11-08) |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum | Two-channel rating at 0.1% THD or tighter for 6-ohm-plus speakers, with no measured or inherited protection fault | Denon AVR-X2900H, $1,349 (95 W, 0.08%) |
| Good | Minimum, plus RCA pre-outs for all five main channels and the sub | Yamaha RX-A4A, $1,599.95 (110 W, 0.06%; 7.2 pre-outs) |
| Best | A measured amplifier with no fault, full pre-outs at a stated level, and two or more independently adjustable sub outputs | Denon AVR-X3900H, $1,849 |

## How the candidates rank

This criterion mixes specs and measurements. A measured defect on the model itself ranks below an unmeasured spec sheet; otherwise measured models come first.

### Best for this criterion

- [Denon AVR-X3900H](../products/denon-avr-x3900h.md): 105 W (8 Ω, 0.08%, 2 ch), amplifier rated very good for the class (ASR, 2026-09-17), 11.4 pre-outs rated 2 V with ASR's best result near 1.5 V, and four independent sub outputs.

### Good

- [Denon AVR-X3800H](../products/denon-avr-x3800h.md): amplifier measured as essentially the X3700H's and unchanged in performance (ASR, 2022-10-27), but pre-outs and sub-output independence are uncaptured and its top ran hot with the fan idle.
- [Marantz CINEMA 50 Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-50-series-2.md): 110 W (0.08%), 11.4 pre-outs and four sub outputs with independent level and distance; unmeasured, with the pre-out voltage unclear between the manual's 1.2 V and 2 V figures.
- [Arcam AVA15](../products/arcam-ava15.md): the only candidate besides NAD publishing multichannel power, 85 W with seven channels and 65 W with nine (8 Ω, 1 kHz, 0.5%), 12 RCA pre-outs at 2 V and up to three sub outputs; unmeasured.
- [Anthem MRX 540 8K](../products/anthem-mrx-540-8k.md): five amp channels, 100 W on a loose 1% basis, 7.2 pre-outs, and two paralleled sub outputs with their own delay and level; unmeasured, and the MRX 1140 sibling never shut down under ASR's test.
- [Yamaha RX-A4A](../products/yamaha-rx-a4a.md): 110 W at a strict 0.06%, 7.2 RCA pre-outs with no stated voltage, two same-signal sub outputs; unmeasured, and the A6A sibling's limiter engaged at seven channels driven.

### Weaker

- [Denon AVR-X2900H](../products/denon-avr-x2900h.md): 95 W (0.08%) and no known fault, but "Pre-out 2.2" most plausibly means fronts plus two subs, which would rule out an external amplifier for the center and surrounds (inference).
- [Marantz CINEMA 70s Series 2](../products/marantz-cinema-70s-series-2.md): the lowest rating here at 50 W, two same-signal sub outputs, pre-outs stated only on the info sheet, and a Series 1 that latched distortion and protection at 4 ohms (ASR, 2023-12-11).
- [Onkyo TX-RZ50](../products/onkyo-tx-rz50.md): 120 W FTC rating, but a measured 4-ohm limiter that latches until the AC cord is pulled, repeated across the platform to 2026 and unaddressed by firmware through 2026-03-03; its 11.2 variable pre-outs (sub 1 V rms) make it usable as a processor with an external amplifier, as ASR recommends for the platform.

### Not enough evidence to rank

- [NAD T 758 V3i](../products/nad-t-758-v3i.md): the strictest published rating, 7 × 60 W with all channels driven at 0.05%, and a V3 predecessor that held 50 W into 4 ohms without pulling back (ASR, 2019-09-07), but pre-out and sub-output counts are unknown; a V3i manual would place it.
- [Marantz CINEMA 50](../products/marantz-cinema-50.md): 110 W with no stated conditions, and pre-outs and sub outputs uncaptured; a Series 1 manual or spec sheet would place it.

## Open questions

Reader-facing list: [open questions](../reference/open-questions.md). These would change the ranking:

- **Speaker impedance and sensitivity** (#4). With 8-ohm, sensitive speakers the RZ50's limiter may never be reached, and the Weaker tier would move up.
- **How often the Onkyo/Pioneer limiter triggers in 5.1 use, and whether firmware fixes it** (#6, #14). Owner reports run both ways.
- **Whether the CINEMA 70s Series 2 inherits the Series 1 fault** (#26). A clean measurement would lift it into Good.
- **Sub-output independence on the RZ50 and the AVA15's Sub 2 and Sub 3** (#27, #30), and the X3800H's and X2900H's pre-outs (#21).
- **Pre-out voltage** is stated only for the X3900H, CINEMA 50 Series 2, RZ50 and AVA15, so matching a future amplifier's input sensitivity is unchecked on the rest.
